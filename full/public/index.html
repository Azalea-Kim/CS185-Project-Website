About IdeaScape

1
Collect, revisit, and reinterpret your visual references with AI support.

In ImageSpace, browse and annotate your personal collection of precedents. Each time you revisit an image, you can add a new interpretation, while AI helps generate tags from your annotations — capturing how your thinking evolves across projects and over time.

2
Search through meaning — not just images

In SearchSpace, using AI-generated tags based on your own words and annotation history, IdeaScape helps surface precedents that are conceptually relevant to your current idea — without guessing the right keyword.

3
Turn references into ideas

In WorkSpace, use and re-use tons of responsive sections to create the perfect layout.
